# OmaDoomed

## Unreleased

- Your channel ring (Drain Life, Drain Soul, Drain Mana, Wrack) is 1.25x the size of a DoT ring, over the mob and on the target frame.
- Crowd control whose mob isn't your target or is off screen now shows as a big teal ring with the spell's name, centred under your character just above the action bars, one ring per hold side by side. It no longer stands beside the target frame. A place you saved for the old spot is reset; move it again with the Move button if you like.
- Fixed: your DoT sizes learned during a fight with Curse of the Elements up kept the boost, so the next cursed fight counted it twice and the skull could show early. Amplify Curse had the same problem on Agony. Learned sizes are now your plain damage, and each boost is added once.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Your target's health bar shows the health your DoTs will still take, one piece per DoT in its own colour.
- The same pieces show over every mob you've dotted, on its nameplate.
- Pieces slide down as the DoTs tick. While you cast a DoT, its piece shows dim and charges up.
- A skull and bones on the target when your DoTs will finish it.
- DoT rings on enemy nameplates. Pick where they sit: above every enemy, on the target frame, or both (Haunt). In Haunt your curse, your drain and your crowd control stay over the mob.
- A DoT in its last seconds flares in its own colour and breathes, on its ring and on the kill bar.
- Two ring looks, Modern and Classic.
- Crowd-control timers: your Fear, Banish, Howl of Terror and your Sayaad's Seduction as a teal ring over the mob. When the mob isn't your target or has run off screen, the ring stands beside your target frame, so you always see how long is left. `/omadoomed cc test` shows one.
- Settings page under Options > AddOns > Oma > OmaDoomed, with a live preview.
- Bug reports: a key binding or `/omadoomed report` saves the last minute of what OmaDoomed drew, with a screenshot.
